# Data attribution and rights

PPG-DaLiA is by Attila Reiss, Ina Indlekofer and Philip Schmidt (2019), distributed through the UCI Machine Learning Repository, DOI [10.24432/C53890](https://doi.org/10.24432/C53890). The [official dataset page](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/495/ppg%2Bdalia), checked on 9 November 2026, states a [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.

This repository contains a small signal excerpt for Figures 1–2 and derived labels, activity-level metrics, validation histories and prediction excerpts. The excerpt preserves the native PPG, wrist ACC and chest ECG and adds the disclosed preprocessing. The predictions and metrics are derived study outputs. The complete participant recordings remain available from UCI and are not copied here. Retain this dataset attribution when sharing these materials. The dataset creators do not endorse the analysis.
